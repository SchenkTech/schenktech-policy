Cracks - Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 2, 2026

SchenkTech ("we", "us", or "our") built Cracks ("the App") as a free application with optional in-app purchases. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use the App.

Information Collection and Use

Cracks does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App runs entirely on your device and does not require an account, login, or any personal data to function.

Game Center

Cracks integrates with Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. Game Center is operated by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your Game Center account information beyond what Apple provides through the standard Game Center interface (such as your player alias and scores).

In-App Purchases

Cracks offers optional in-app purchases processed through Apple's App Store. All payment information is handled by Apple and is not accessible to us. Purchases are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

Cracks may offer optional rewarded video ads. These ads are opt-in only and are never shown without your action. Ad networks may collect device-level data as described in their respective privacy policies. No ads are shown without your explicit consent.

iCloud Sync

Cracks supports optional iCloud sync to keep your progress consistent across devices. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is not accessible to us. iCloud is operated by Apple and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

Your game progress (level completion, scores, stars, achievements, and settings) is saved locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to any server and is not accessible to us.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.
