Cascade!

Chain Reactions in Every Swipe

Cascade! is a sliding number puzzle where consecutive numbers merge. Swipe to slide all tiles — when a 3 lands next to a 4, they merge into 7. When that 7 lands next to an 8, they merge into 15. A single swipe can set off a chain of merges. That's a cascade.

How to Play

  • Swipe in any direction to slide all tiles simultaneously.
  • Two tiles merge when they are consecutive integers — one apart in value (e.g. 3 & 4, 7 & 8, 15 & 16).
  • After a merge, if the resulting tile is now consecutive with its new neighbor, they merge too — creating a chain reaction.
  • A new tile spawns after each swipe that moves at least one tile.
  • The game ends when the board is full and no consecutive pairs remain adjacent.

Game Modes

Campaign

120 hand-crafted levels across 10 worlds. Each world introduces a new challenge. Earn up to 3 stars per level. Unlock new worlds by earning enough stars across previous ones.

Classic

Endless play on a 4×4 grid. Swipe until the board fills and no moves remain. Your goal: reach the highest tile value and best score you can.

Daily Challenge

One shared puzzle per day. Everyone plays the same board. Scores are posted to the global Game Center leaderboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why don't equal tiles merge?

Cascade! uses a consecutive-integer rule, not an equal-value rule. Only tiles that differ by exactly 1 merge (e.g. 3+4=7, not 4+4=8). This is the core mechanic that makes Cascade! different from 2048-style games.

How do I unlock new worlds in Campaign?

Each world (except the first) requires a minimum total star count from earlier worlds to unlock. Replay earlier levels to earn more stars if you're stuck.

How are stars awarded?

Each campaign level awards 1, 2, or 3 stars based on how efficiently you complete it — fewer moves earns more stars.

Does Cascade! work offline?

Yes. All game modes work fully offline. iCloud sync and Game Center leaderboards require a connection but are never required to play.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings (gear icon on the main screen) and tap Reset All Data. You will be asked to confirm before anything is deleted. This also clears iCloud sync data for this app.

My score didn't appear on the leaderboard — what happened?

Leaderboard scores require an active Game Center connection. If you were offline when you finished, the score will be submitted automatically the next time you open the app with a connection.
