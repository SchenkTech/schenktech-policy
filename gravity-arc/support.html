About Gravity Arc

Gravity Arc is a physics-based puzzle game where you place gravity wells to redirect a projectile toward a target zone. Arrange gravitational objects during the placement phase, then watch physics resolve your solution during the simulation.

Common Questions

  • How do I place gravity wells? — Tap to place a gravity well, drag to reposition it, and use the slider to adjust its mass.
  • What is the dashed line? — That's the trajectory preview showing the predicted path of the projectile before you launch.
  • How does scoring work? — You earn a base score of 150 points plus bonuses for accuracy, speed, efficiency (unused wells), and single-well solutions. Star thresholds vary by level.
  • How do hints work? — After 3 failed attempts on a level, a hint becomes available showing a ghost well at the correct position.
  • What is Zen Mode? — An endless mode with procedurally-generated puzzles where difficulty scales with your streak count.
  • What are Daily and Weekly Challenges? — A new level each day (200 points) and a harder level each week (500 points). The same level is available to all players.
  • What are the advanced mechanics? — Later worlds introduce wormholes, pulse wells, danger zones (black holes), gravity walls, timed wells, dual targets, shifting wells, and repulsive wells.
  • Is there a colorblind mode? — Yes. In settings, you can enable colorblind mode (replaces red/green with orange/blue) and high contrast mode.
  • Is my progress saved? — Yes, your progress is saved automatically on your device and can sync across devices via iCloud.
  • Does Gravity Arc require an internet connection? — No. An internet connection is only needed for Game Center leaderboards and iCloud sync.
